the two writes together broke the rule. Snapshot isolation cannot see
this because the anomaly is across rows, not inside one.

The retry re-ran the same code, read the total of 1 left by the winner,
and refused the sale. One unit was sold, one remains, and the invariant
held. The price of SERIALIZABLE is that the losing transaction must be
retried by the application; the price of REPEATABLE READ is a silently
broken rule.
